Connection

From the Square Developer Console: create an application, open its Production credentials and copy the access token. It needs PAYMENTS_READ, PAYOUTS_READ, INVOICES_READ and MERCHANT_PROFILE_READ; a personal access token has them.
An account for the Square balance works best, so payouts show as transfers to your bank.
