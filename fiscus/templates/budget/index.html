How budget lines work

A category's budget is the whole amount. Bills in it do not add to it: when you tick one as paid on the Bills page, it comes off the budget as spent. The forecast spends what is budgeted beyond the bills, since the bills are forecast from their own dates.

For something paid every few months or once a year (rego, insurance), choose how often. Its monthly share is saved up and what you spend comes out of that, so the month you pay it is not over budget. What it holds counts as still to spend, so it is never free in your accounts. Set what it holds with Saved now, or, when a bill in the year ahead would find it short, Set saved to puts in just enough to have every bill on its due date.

Saving for something, like a holiday? Put it in a goal: what a goal holds comes off what is free in your accounts, not out of the budget. A goal can also save from budget lines (utilities, yearly bills): a Put aside button on each line then spends from it and puts the money in the goal at once.

Groups put budget lines under headings with their own totals. Choose a line's group when you edit it. Tap a group's name to hide its lines and see just the totals.

Your payday works well. A day past the end of a short month falls on its last day.
