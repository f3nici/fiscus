Add a budget line

Saving for something, like a holiday? Put it in a goal: what a goal holds comes off what is free in your accounts, not out of the budget. For money you budget each month but pay every few months (utilities, yearly bills), give the goal its budget lines to save from (one goal can take several), and a Put aside button here spends it from each line and puts it in the goal at once.

For something paid every few months or once a year (rego, insurance), choose how often. Its monthly share is saved up and what you spend comes out of that, so the month you pay it is not over budget.

Import from a spreadsheet

An Excel (.xlsx) or CSV budget with Item, Amount and Frequency columns. Section headings and total rows are recognised; you check every line before anything is saved. CSV template

The budget you set for a category is the whole amount. Bills in that category do not add to it: when you tick one as paid on the Bills page, it comes off the budget as spent. The forecast spends what is budgeted beyond the bills, since the bills are forecast from their own dates.
