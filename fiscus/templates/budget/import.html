Each ticked line becomes a budget for its category. The amount is what is spent each time, as often as How often says. Changing How often keeps the yearly cost: $1,200 every year becomes $200 every 2 months, $100 a month either way. A line spent every few months is saved up a month at a time, so the month you pay it is not over budget. Lines that share a category are added together, and bills in a category are paid out of its budget, not added to it. Nothing is saved until you press Import.
