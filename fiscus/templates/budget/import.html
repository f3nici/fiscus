Each ticked line becomes a budget for its category. A line paid every 2, 3 or 6 months or yearly is saved up a month at a time, so the month you pay it is not over budget; change How often if the file has it wrong. The amount to budget is always a month's share: a yearly $1,200 is $100 a month. Lines that share a category are added together (the category is saved up if any of them is), and bills in a category are paid out of its budget, not added to it. Change a category, an amount or how often before importing; nothing is saved until you press Import.
