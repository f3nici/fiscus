Gross and tax are for the tax estimate. Left blank, it uses this job's payslips once there are some, and until then counts the pay as having no tax taken out, as for contract work.

Irregular is for pay with no set days, such as pay from your own business. Put in each payment as it comes. The budget, forecast and tax estimate expect a typical month: the average of the last year, or what you put here until it has paid anything.

Pay that varies, or irregular pay, can expect what it paid the same month last year instead, from its payslips, so a quiet January or a busy December shows as one. A payday with no payslip counts as nothing paid, and a month from before its first payslip expects the average.
